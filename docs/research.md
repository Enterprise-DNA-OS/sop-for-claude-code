# Research notes

Checked 14 November 2026.

- Kinaxis publishes no price for Maestro (formerly RapidResponse). It is sold by custom quote. Third-party estimates (Toolradar, https://toolradar.com/tools/kinaxis/pricing) put annual licensing at US$250,000 to US$1,000,000 or more for 10 to 100 users, before implementation. These are estimates, not invoices.
- Kinaxis names CSR (building products, Australia and New Zealand) as its first Australia-based corporate customer: https://www.kinaxis.com/en/node/4006
- The monthly cycle used here (data gathering, demand review, supply review, pre-S&OP, executive meeting) is the standard five-step S&OP process taught in operations planning courses and used across planning software.
